How the verdicts work

  • Stop the loss / Protect gains: loss past {{ ss.stop_loss_pct | num(0) }}%, a broken trend, the trailing stop hit, a broken thesis, analyst downgrades, insider selling or SEC red flags. Called "protect gains" when you are still in profit.
  • Take profit: a gain of {{ ss.take_profit_pct | num(0) }}% or more, price at or above the analyst target, or overbought. Suggests selling a quarter to a third and letting the rest run.
  • Trim to cap: the position is above the {{ ss.position_cap_pct | num(0) }}% cap. Suggests the sale that brings it back to the cap.
  • The trailing stop sits twice the stock's normal one-month swing below its 6-month high (2 × one-month volatility, kept between 12% and 30%).
  • Dots show how strong each reason is (●●● strongest). "Strong" means the reasons add up to {{ ss.strong_score }} or more.
  • Holdings with a price or data problem get no suggestion until it's fixed.
